Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Captured by Sienna website (“we,” “us,” the “Site”) handles information when you visit. Captured by Sienna is an independent photography service based in Marion, New York.

1. Information we collect directly

The Site itself does not have accounts, contact forms, or trackers that we operate. We do not directly collect names, emails, or payment details through this website.

2. Third-party services

The Site uses services run by other companies. When you interact with them, those companies — not us — collect and process your data under their own privacy policies:

  • Instagram / Meta — the Site links to our Instagram profile. If you visit it, Meta may set cookies and collect usage data. See Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — the Site is hosted by GitHub, which may log basic request data (such as IP address) for security. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

3. Client galleries

If we share a private gallery link with you, the photos in it are hosted on GitHub Pages. Anyone with the link can view that gallery, so please keep your link private. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to view it.

4. Cookies

We do not set our own cookies. Linked third-party content may set cookies on your device. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Children’s privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Minors photographed for a session are consented to by a parent or guardian.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted here.
